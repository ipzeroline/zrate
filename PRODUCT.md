# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday people across Southeast Asia — Thailand, Laos, Myanmar, Cambodia — checking live exchange rates before exchanging cash, sending money across borders, or planning purchases in foreign currencies. They use the site on mobile phones, often on variable connections. They need speed above all: open the page, see the rate, convert, done. Many are not tech-savvy; they recognize flags and currency codes but not financial jargon.

## Product Purpose

zrate.io gives people in SE Asia a fast, reliable, multilingual currency converter that just works. One page. Every major currency. Five languages. Updated every 60 seconds. The site also serves static SEO pages for individual currency pairs so people searching "USD to THB today" land directly on a readable result.

## Brand Personality

**Refined, Trusted, Effortless.**

The site should feel like a premium financial tool — quiet confidence, nothing flashy, everything exactly where you expect it. Trust comes from precision and clarity, not from institutional heaviness or cyber aesthetics. Effortless means the interface disappears into the task: open, see, convert, leave.

## Anti-references

- Traditional bank websites: cluttered rate tables, institutional blue, heavy navigation, information overload
- The current neon-cyber aesthetic: glow effects, dark-only, terminal fonts, decorative sci-fi elements
- Crypto-exchange dashboards: dark mode only, data-overdose, trading-terminal energy

The sweet spot is a premium personal finance tool — something that feels as considered as a high-end banking app but stays fast and utilitarian for everyday use.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed is the feature.** Every design choice must serve faster rate lookup. No decoration that slows the user down.
2. **Clarity builds trust.** Rates must be immediately readable. No ambiguity in currency labels, no visual noise around the numbers that matter.
3. **Works everywhere.** Mobile-first, readable on slow connections, functional in all five languages with their varying text lengths.
4. **Quietly premium.** Quality shows in typography, spacing, and restraint — not in effects. The site earns trust by feeling solid, not by shouting.
5. **One surface, one job.** The homepage is a converter. The pair pages are reference sheets. Nothing competes with the primary task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum across all surfaces
- Must work on low-end mobile devices and patchy 3G connections
- Five languages with right-to-left safety (though none currently RTL)
- Color not the sole differentiator for any state (rate changes, status indicators, favorites)
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
